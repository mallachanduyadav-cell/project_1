Our Story

A small cafe, run like family

Chandu's is a compact, no-frills spot near the bus stand — not a big dining hall, just a clean, honest kitchen turning out fresh tiffins, meals and filter coffee. We keep things simple so daily commuters, pilgrims heading to Tirumala, and neighbourhood regulars can eat well without waiting long.
